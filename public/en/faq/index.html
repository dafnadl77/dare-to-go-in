What is DARE TO GO IN?

DARE TO GO IN is a private space for returning to the dreams that stay with us. You tell a dream by voice or in writing, see it in an image, choose the moment that stayed with you, and receive a personal reflection and questions to think about. You can save each dream in your own journal.

How does the process work?

You tell the dream. An image is created from it, and if it is not accurate you can correct it. You choose the one moment that stayed with you most and write what comes to mind when you think about it. If you like, you choose up to three feelings. Then a personal reflection and a short summary are created, drawing on the dream, the moment, your association and the feelings you chose.

Does the AI really know what the dream means?

No. Nobody can know for certain what a dream "means". The AI offers possibilities, connections and questions, not truth and not a diagnosis. What feels right is what is right for you.

Can I record a dream?

Yes. Press and hold the circle and tell the dream. You can also type it. If there is no microphone on the device, or access to it is blocked, you can write the dream instead. To turn the recording into text it is sent for transcription (see the Privacy Policy).

Why choose a moment and feelings?

One moment that stayed with you is a personal point of view: the reflection is built around it rather than around every detail of the dream. The feelings help tell apart what happened in the dream from what you felt afterwards, and both go into the reflection. The feelings are always your own report, and the reflection does not draw conclusions from them.

Can I skip choosing feelings?

Yes. It is optional: you can carry on without choosing, and then the reflection is built from the dream, the moment and the association alone. The feelings are sent in the same request as the reflection, with no extra charge, and are stored with the dream only if you choose to save it.

Is the first dream free?

Yes. When you sign up you get one free dream, one per account. You join with a short sign-up (email and password, or a Google account) and then you start telling.

Do I need a monthly subscription?

No. There is no monthly subscription on the site. You choose a package of a number of dreams and pay for it on a secure Grow payment page.

What happens when the dreams in my package run out?

You can still open and read all your saved dreams. To tell a new dream you need another package.

Are my dreams kept if I do not buy another package?

Yes. A saved dream stays in your journal, in your account, until you delete it or the account.

Who can see my dreams?

A saved dream is tied to your account. To create an image and a reflection, the text of the dream (and the transcript of a recording, if you recorded) is sent to the AI provider, OpenAI. We do not sell the content and do not use it to train a model of our own. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Can I delete a dream or an account?

Yes. You can delete a dream from the archive. An account is deleted from Settings ("Delete account"), which removes the account, all dreams and images, the reflections and any unused dreams in a package. This cannot be undone.

Can I export my dreams to PDF?

Export to PDF is not available yet. You can ask for a copy of your data by writing to daretogoin@gmail.com.

Can I use it on a computer and a phone?

Yes. The site works in the browser on a computer, a tablet and a phone, with the same account. There is no app to download.

What if the payment went through but my dreams were not added?

The dreams are added to your account automatically once the payment is confirmed, which can take a few minutes. If time has passed and they have not appeared, write to daretogoin@gmail.com with the email address of your account and the reference shown on the screen you return to after paying, and we will check.

Does the reflection replace professional help?

No. The reflection is a tool for personal thought, not a diagnosis, advice or treatment. If things are hard, please reach out to a licensed professional.
